From street lead to confirmed holiday

This guide follows one new member through the whole system: the first conversation, the membership application, payments, a holiday booking and the paperwork after it. Each chapter says who signs in, what to click and what to look for. Every screenshot was taken from the running application.

Before you start

Demo environment and roles

All names, phone numbers, PANs and payments in the demo are fictitious. Prices, ASF and GST rates are labelled DEMO values and are set by the business in Admin → Master data & rates.

Each person sees only the menus and buttons their role allows, and the server enforces the same rules. To show this, sign in as different users during the demo. The project team will give you the shared demo password separately.

Chapter 1

Sign in, dashboard and search

Sign in as demo.sales (any user works)

What to click

  1. Sign in with username and password.
  2. The Dashboard opens on what needs attention today.
  3. Click any tile to open the matching filtered list.
  4. Press Ctrl + K and type a membership number, lead ID, name or mobile.

What to point out

  • Six tiles: follow-ups due, approvals pending, overdue EMIs, reservations waiting on ASF, default notices expiring, upcoming check-ins.
  • Search accepts any format: 9001, MSMC8-9001, or a mobile with or without +91.
  • The orange DEV badge appears on test environments only, never in production.
Chapter 2

Capture a lead

Sign in as demo.sales or demo.marketing

What to click

  1. Leads → New lead. Enter name, mobile, email, city and the OPC's name. Click Save lead.
  2. On the lead, click Mark contacted.
  3. Enter the presentation date and time, then Mark presentation scheduled.
  4. After the presentation, click Attended.

What to point out

  • Each lead gets an ID such as L-2026-000002.
  • A matching mobile or email triggers a possible-duplicate warning. Saving anyway needs a reason, and the reason is logged.
  • Every status change is kept in the history at the bottom of the lead. Notes are added, never overwritten.
Chapter 3

Convert the lead to a member

Still demo.sales

The application screen follows the paper membership form from top to bottom, so staff can key it in without hunting for fields.

What to click

  1. On the attended lead, click Convert to member. Name, mobile and email are already filled in.
  2. Purchaser details: date of birth, PAN, address, PIN code, state.
  3. Membership details: brand CLUB8, Premium, Red season, Studio, 7 nights a year, 10 years.
  4. Payment details: price ₹2,50,000, admin fees ₹5,000, down payment ₹55,000 by card. Then EMI ₹20,000 × 10 from next month.
  5. Tick the signatures and click Create member & convert lead.

What to point out

  • Total (₹2,55,000.00) and balance (₹2,00,000.00) are calculated as you type, and the last year of use is filled in automatically.
  • Only the last 4 digits of a card are ever stored. For Aadhaar, only the last 4 digits and a masked copy are kept.
  • The membership number (MSMC8-…) is assigned automatically. The member starts as Pending approval.
  • The lead is kept, marked Converted and linked to the new member.
Chapter 4

Approve the membership

Upload as demo.sales, approve as demo.manager

What to click

  1. On the member, open Correspondence & Documents and upload the signed agreement (type "Membership Agreement (signed)").
  2. Sign in as the manager and open the member's Profile.
  3. The approval panel shows "All checks passed". Click Approve member.
  4. Open the Entitlement tab.

What to point out

  • The panel lists anything missing, such as the down payment, signed agreement, KYC or mandatory fields. Approval is blocked until they are done.
  • On approval the member becomes Active and 7 nights are credited for each of the 10 years.
  • The header always shows nights left this year, outstanding balance, next check-in and this year's ASF status.
Chapter 5

Payments, EMIs and receipts

Sign in as demo.finance

What to click

  1. Open the member's Finance tab. The card down payment is already in the ledger with a receipt.
  2. Click Generate EMI schedule: 10 instalments of ₹20,000.
  3. Click Record payment. Choose Membership EMI, ₹20,000, UPI, enter the reference and save.
  4. Click Receipt on the ledger row to open the PDF.

What to point out

  • Instalment 1 changes to Paid. Payments are applied to the oldest instalments first, and any overpayment is kept as credit.
  • Cash is not offered as a payment method. A reference is required for every payment.
  • Payments are never edited. Correct posts a reversal or adjustment with a reason, so the history stays complete.
  • GST appears only on the ASF, shown separately from the base amount.
Chapter 6

Book a stay, pay the ASF, confirm

Sign in as demo.reservations, with demo.finance for the ASF

This is the centre of the demo. The system works out the entitlement and the annual fee before anything is submitted, and it will not confirm a booking while the year's ASF is unpaid.

What to click

  1. On the member, open Reservations → New reservation.
  2. Choose Goa → Demo Beach Resort, dates about 3 weeks ahead, 4 nights. Watch the calculation on the right.
  3. Click Submit request, then open the new reservation (R-2026-…).
  4. Click Approve (deduct entitlement). Enter the property's confirmation number and click Confirm. It is refused.
  5. As finance: Finance → Pay ASF 2026, card, reference, save.
  6. Back as reservations: Confirm now works. Click Generate confirmation / voucher PDF.

What to point out

  • Before submitting, staff see the nights used and which years they come from (saved, current or borrowed), plus the ASF due: ₹12,000 + 5% GST = ₹12,600.00 at the demo rate.
  • Nights are held when requested and deducted exactly once on approval. Clicking again cannot deduct twice.
  • The ASF is raised at the first booking in a membership year. A second booking in the same year shows "ASF payable: none".
  • The entitlement now shows 4 nights used and 3 remaining for 2026.
Confirmation is blocked until the ASF is paid

The agreement says non-payment of ASF results in non-confirmation, so the server refuses Confirm. A user with the override permission can confirm anyway with a written reason, which is recorded.

Chapter 7

Emails, calls and documents

Sign in as demo.service

What to click

  1. Open the member's Correspondence & Documents tab.
  2. Log an email: subject "Early check-in request", then Add email.
  3. Log a call: summary "Welcome call", then Add call.
  4. Upload a PDF and click Preview.

What to point out

  • Emails can also be imported from .eml or .msg files, and replies are threaded automatically.
  • The timeline shows everything about the member in date order and can be filtered by type.
  • Documents open in a viewer through a short-lived link. Deleting one needs a reason.
Chapter 8

Every change on record

Sign in as demo.manager, then demo.admin

What to click

  1. On the member's Profile, click Edit, change the comments and give a reason. Save.
  2. Open the History tab.
  3. As admin, open Activity log and click Verify integrity.

What to point out

  • Each change shows who made it, when, the reason, and the old and new values side by side.
  • The history covers every step of this demo: lead, conversion, approval, payments, ASF, entitlement, reservation, emails, calls and uploads.
  • The activity log is chained like a ledger, so any tampering is detected. It can be filtered and exported to Excel.
Chapter 9 · optional

Everyday lists and administration

Lists can be filtered, saved as searches and exported. Admin covers users, roles, a permission grid, and master data such as rates, which are added as new dated rows so the old rate stays on record.

Chapter 10 · optional

On the phone

The same application works on a phone browser, so a salesperson on the street can look up a member or lead in seconds.

Rules at a glance

Every rule below is enforced by the server, not just the screen. Figures marked as settings are master data the business can change.

AreaRule
ASFOne fee per membership year, raised at the first booking that uses that year's nights, plus 5% GST. It is paid in full in one payment.
ConfirmationBlocked while the ASF for the years used is unpaid. An override needs the permission and a reason.
Saving nightsUnused nights can be saved for up to 2 years (a setting). Expired nights are recorded, not deleted.
BorrowingUp to 2 future years (a setting), never beyond the last year of use.
DeductionNights are held at request and deducted once on approval. A season and category matrix converts booked nights to entitlement nights.
CancellationNeeds a reason. A confirmed booking also needs the member's written request. Nights credited back: more than 30 days' notice 100%, 15–30 days 50%, under 15 days none.
RescheduleOriginal dates are kept in the history, and nights are adjusted, never deducted twice. A third reschedule needs a manager.
PaymentsNo cash. A reference is required, and a receipt PDF is produced. Mistakes are corrected with reversals, never edits.
DefaultOverdue EMIs can trigger a default notice with a 14-day deadline. Paying in time returns the member to Active.
RatesAdded as new dated rows. ASF increases above 5% need an override reason and special permission.
Personal dataCard numbers: last 4 digits only. Aadhaar: last 4 digits and a masked copy. Viewing full KYC numbers is logged and hides again after 30 seconds.
Two brands, one system

Club8 Holidays and K8 share one CRM. Each member record carries its brand, and receipts, notices and vouchers print with that brand's logo, colours and legal entity.
